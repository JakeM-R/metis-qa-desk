      How it scores
      
    
        
    Four fixed axes (correctness, guideline adherence, completeness, format) plus up to four hard rules from the spec. Correctness checks every amount, date and status against the tool results. Quotes are copied from the output, never written.
    
        
    Two broken rules or a mean under 2.5: reject. One broken rule or any axis under 3: rework. A rule the task never triggers is n/a.
    
        
    A rules engine in the browser: no model, no server. In production a calibrated model grader would replace the matching step; the rubric, quotes and rule stay.

        How the numbers work
        
      
          
      SLAs: Queued 1 day, Labelling 4, QA 1, Rework 2. Cost is tasks × rate, with rework paid again. Per accepted divides the cost of reviewed tasks by the accepted ones.
      
          
      An active batch is projected from its current pace plus one QA pass. "This week" is every active batch plus anything delivered in the last seven days. The tracker saves to this browser only.
